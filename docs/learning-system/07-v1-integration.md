# V1 integration and operations

Status: proposed plan, not implementation authorization. [Plan index](README.md).

## Outcome

Two complete learning loops operating with recovery and inspection.

Dependencies: Steps 1–6.

Roadmap clarification (2026-11-05, updated after the step 4 interview): [post-audit 3](POST-AUDIT-03-broaden-experience-understanding.md) owns the implemented intake, inquiry, and initial product/delivery foundation; [post-audit 4](POST-AUDIT-04-broader-learning-products.md) completes remaining broader-product semantics and validation. Reuse both rather than duplicate their work. Their relevant behavioral gates remain prerequisites for later training and integration claims; ordinary-experience understanding is not deferred to later expansion.

## Work

- Exercise conversational correction from evidence through adaptation, dataset export, evaluation, and promotion.
- Exercise game prediction, execution, assessment, and skill/hypothesis revision.
- Verify leased jobs, idempotency, stale-result rejection, resource scheduling, and recovery. Respect post-audit 3's no-spending-cap decision; progress management and mechanical execution limits must not introduce a learning quota.
- Show why a behavior changed, which lessons/models were used, and how to withdraw or roll back changes.
- Distinguish stopping new learning from disabling use of already accepted learning.

## Decisions to settle within this plan

Operational budgets; degraded-mode behavior; rollout scope; minimum scenario coverage; release checklist.

## Completion evidence

Retries do not duplicate trials; stale work cannot restore withdrawn lessons; failures remain visible; user-scoped records remain isolated; both end-to-end loops meet their agreed acceptance criteria.
