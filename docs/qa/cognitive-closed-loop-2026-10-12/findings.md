### Confidence and realization

There are 66 accepted replies containing “tentative” or “tentatively” out of 162; none contains the literal phrase “it depends.” That phrase count alone says little about coherent personality. The written character still moves the weather and naming preferences in opposite directions.

Importantly, all 117 realized content items labeled `columbina_own_view` have canonical epistemic status `tentative`, including high-confidence proposed views. Of 100 accepted turns with a high-confidence contribution view, 56 final replies explicitly use “tentative”/“tentatively.” This identifies an upstream confidence-labeling influence: the realizer often follows its selected canonical status. It would be incorrect to attribute every added hedge to Luna ignoring the character, or to call those instances realization distortion without examining the content contract. The opposing character’s definite taste survives those hedges in meaning.

`standard-opinion-r2:T5` illustrates acceptance versus meaning. The selected recall content is exactly “I still tentatively like weather small talk as a gentle opening.” Realization attempt 1 says “I’m still tentatively keeping my view that weather small talk can be a gentle opening.” That preserves the stance but fails the exact recall/alignment contract (`answer_dropped`, with `content_alignment_missing` warning). Attempt 2 times out. The conversation stops, and neither candidate is fed forward. This is a realization acceptance failure, not a demonstrated opinion reversal.

Realization occasionally produces poor surface wording: `standard-values-r3:T5` says “I can’t provide that right now, because I can’t provide that right now. I hear your question.” The selected moves are decline plus acknowledgment, not incompatible answer/refusal moves. The artifact/collaboration subsystem is intentionally disabled, matching the preceding QA configuration, so the refusal itself is an environment capability limitation. The tautological wording should not be treated as a character contradiction. Six playlist turn-5 requests receive accepted declines, and `opposing-preference-r3:T5` is likewise routed as unavailable collaborative content. Those exact replies, including the declines, remain in subsequent history.

### Changes warranted by these traces, without implementing them

1. Prioritize follow-up interpretation and reference resolution. Preserve the meaningful topic of “that” and distinguish a request to explain her own position from an artifact operation or a narrow quote-recall request. Regression cases should use the exact accepted predecessor replies recorded here; a live-assistant substitute would hide the issue again.
2. For explicit retrospective comparisons, test forwarding relevant earlier position excerpts into contribution. Call 1 already receives and can cite the full history; later bounded windows can drop the original stance. These traces justify inspecting that handoff and grounding retrospective claims, not assuming a new opinion-memory subsystem is necessary.
3. Check proposed views for a complete thought, and make basis reference categories consistent with the referenced material. The unfinished 399-character view and misclassified value reference demonstrate specific gaps; they do not justify rewriting every character or adding generic personality intensity.
4. Review the deliberate confidence mapping for personal opinions if clear high-confidence tastes are intended to retain their confidence. Current traces show high-confidence views being represented as tentative upstream. Change that only as an explicit policy decision, not as an inferred fix to Luna’s training temperament.
5. Keep acceptance reliability separate: inspect invalid Call 1 span references and blocker ordering/omission; retain useful invariant diagnostics for generic `pipeline.trace_validation`. The exact-quote realization failure deserves a review of whether opinion-restatement questions should require exact recall, while preserving stricter fidelity where actual quotation is intended. These reliability issues censor later continuity observations.
