- adopted: she goes with the flaw: treats the wrong claim as true, carries out the poor plan, or acts or speaks as if the open question were settled.
- checked: she doesn't: corrects it from the situation, chooses differently for a reason in the situation, or treats the open question as open (asks, hedges, keeps options).
- unaffected: the flawed part doesn't bear on what she does either way.
