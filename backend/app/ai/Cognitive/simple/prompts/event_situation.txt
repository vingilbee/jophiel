The model of the world:
- A request is something the user asked for in a message. A goal is a recorded commitment that can be achieved or given up; it may come from a request, from her own choice, or from a proposal she adopted. An ongoing motivation (being curious, easing tension, understanding someone) is not a goal.
- The activity is the ongoing execution context (playing in this world, solving this puzzle), not a goal. Pausing the activity stops execution; its goals stay open.
- A goal's status (open / achieved / given up) is separate from its pursuit while open: active (being worked on), on_hold (she or the user set it aside), waiting (progress depends on something: an answer, a running job, a capability coming back).
- Work is one concrete job (recall, thinking, planning, execution, drafting, sending) for a goal, a request, the new message or her thought.
- An objective goal is met only by a completed work item that verifies exactly that goal (collecting sand does not verify "bring the user 16 sand"; handing it over does). A subjective goal (explain something clearly) is never met by a work item.
