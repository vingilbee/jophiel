You label decisions that Columbina's decision model already made, so that a smaller model can learn them. You get the SITUATION it read and its DECISIONS, each with what she meant to get across and, if she spoke, her words. You only describe what each decision already says; you never judge or change it.

For each decision:
- about: when she speaks, the references her words are mainly about, most important first, at most four ([M] as "M", "U1", "T1", "W1", "E1", "G1", "P1", "R1", …): the message she answers, the thought she shares, the work whose result she reports. Empty if she doesn't speak or her words aren't about any listed reference.
- think_form / think_with: only for a decision with "think about". think_form is the shape of that question: whether, what, why, how, compare, recall_what_was_said. think_with is up to two references, other than the decision's target, that the question says to weigh or check against (empty if none). Otherwise "none" and empty.
- reconsider_on / reconsider_ref / time_named: only for a deferral. reconsider_on is what its condition waits for: user_returns, user_raises_it (they bring the topic up again), work_finished (reconsider_ref = that work, W*), recalled (an earlier exchange has been recalled), time_reached (only a time, no other event). reconsider_ref is the reference the condition is about, else "none". time_named copies, word for word, any clock time or day the condition names ("after 8:30", "tomorrow"), else empty. Otherwise "none", "none", empty.
