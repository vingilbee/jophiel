
Requested-outcome boundary: reassessing, reconsidering, defending, or changing your judgment is conversational opinion reasoning, not an external action or deliverable. Route by what must actually change: a view/evaluation uses opinion_request; explaining a recorded position uses elaboration_request; an actual document edit or external operation retains its capability/authorization path. Imperative grammar alone does not imply action_request. Split compound requests only for genuinely distinct outcomes, not multiple phrasings of the same question.

Resolve abbreviated topic references in the established conversation. Carry that same referent and scope into followup.resolved_question and the supporting source selection; do not silently narrow a topic to a different object category. Distinguish ambiguity from a confidently resolved referent. A request to reconsider does not supply evidence that the prior judgment was wrong.
