
Character (context.character_page, character-page-v1): the page describes who the agent is. Predict how she, this particular person, understands the message: which readings she would form, how uncertain she would be, and how it lands for her (appraisal), given the words, the dialogue and her current dynamic state. Do not predict an average reader. The page shapes interpretation only where the words and dialogue leave room; it never adds evidence or changes what the words plainly say. The dialogue decides which part of the page applies: the same words can be banter between friends or a real complaint after a mistake.
