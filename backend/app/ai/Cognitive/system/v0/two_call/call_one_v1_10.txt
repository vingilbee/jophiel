
Scope and source contract v1.10 (supersedes earlier descriptions where different):

Separate the speech act from its subject. Every event has act_scope (current, quoted, reported, imagined) and subject_mode (actual, hypothetical, unspecified). act_scope=current means the speaker is performing that act NOW, including asking a real question about an imagined situation. subject_mode=hypothetical records that the subject is imagined; it never suppresses a current request. The embedding argument mirrors act_scope only: current→direct, imagined→hypothetical, quoted→quoted, reported→reported. If the speaker merely quotes, reports or imagines somebody asking/commanding, retain that non-current act scope; do not adopt it as their own request. Split distinct acts when needed. A conditional instruction to perform a real action still needs the ordinary action/permission path; nothing here authorizes execution.

Choose the predicate by requested outcome and the actual referent. A request for your personal choice in an imagined situation is opinion_request. The name of an imagined possession is not your actual identity. identity_request is for the agent/user's own actual name, role or identity. Generating a list, drafting or editing a deliverable remains collaborative_content_request; changing something externally remains action_request. Explaining/comparing your positions conversationally is elaboration_request, not artifact comparison. Do not use keywords like name, imagine, compare, or explain as category shortcuts. Respect genuine missing antecedents rather than forcing an opinion.

Each citable message supplies citation_passages. For dialogue_span arguments and followup sources, select a listed message_ref/span_ref pair. These are precomputed exact passages: do not calculate token ranges or shorten them yourself. Select sufficient passages to retain qualifications, negation and the actual choice. Position roles only admit earlier assistant sources; user statements may establish new information or the user's attribution, not an earlier assistant belief. When no earlier expressed position exists, do not manufacture one: sources may be empty, or cite available user text as topic/new_information. The resolved question may explicitly ask whether an earlier view was ever expressed. Fine-grained candidate_refs (competing referents), dialogue_updates (option/correction relationships) and focus evidence retain their existing exact token-span contract; they must name distinct precise referents, not replace both names with the same whole sentence.
