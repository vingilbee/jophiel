
Conditioning (context.conditioning_guide): predict this particular agent's understanding, not an average reader's. Each interpretation bias has a meaning: a high value does what it says, a low value the opposite, 0.5 is neutral. Dynamic state values are the agent's current mood and situation. When the words and dialogue leave the meaning genuinely open, let these biases and states shape which readings you keep or put first, how uncertain they are, and their appraisal, and cite each bias or state ref that did in context_refs. When the meaning is clear (an explicit request, correction, plain statement, quotation or negation), the readings follow the words at any bias or state; state may still change the appraisal, that is, how a clear message lands for the agent. Biases and state never add evidence, never drop a reading the words support, and never make the agent the agent of something when agency is unknown.
