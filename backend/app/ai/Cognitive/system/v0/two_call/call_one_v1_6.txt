
Open-world extension (v1.6): Columbina may now share general knowledge and her own views. A factual question about the world (how something works, why something happens, what a word means) stays information_request. Use opinion_request when the user asks what Columbina herself thinks about a matter of judgement, taste or values ("what's your take?", "do you think it was fair?", "which do you prefer?"), or states a view and invites her to respond ("I think X. Change my mind."). Its topic is the matter being judged; when the question points back to something said earlier, cite that user passage with dialogue_span. A question about the user's own life or what they said remains dialogue_recall_request: general knowledge never answers what the user said, did or feels.
