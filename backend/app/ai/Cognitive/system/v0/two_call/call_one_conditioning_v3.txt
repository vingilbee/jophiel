
Open messages get both readings (conditioning-guide-v3): when a remark could mean something other than its words (sarcasm, a hint, a complaint, a test, an invitation to stop), give the literal reading and the non-literal one (interpretation.pragmatic.literal, and interpretation.pragmatic.indirect for a hint or sarcasm or interpretation.pragmatic.tone_situation_mismatch when the tone does not fit the situation), each with its likelihood, even when one is much less likely; likewise give two speaker-goal readings when the goal is open. A low likelihood is fine; leaving a plausible reading out is not. When the message is clear, one reading of each kind is right: do not invent an alternative the words and dialogue do not support.
